RESEARCH PLAN: LOCATING 'THE HITCHHIKER'S GUIDE TO THE GALAXY'
==============================================================

OBJECTIVE
---------
To locate and acquire a copy of 'The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy' by Douglas Adams
through systematic research across multiple available channels and formats.


BOOK INFORMATION
----------------
Title: The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy
Author: Douglas Adams
Original Publication Date: October 12, 1979
Publisher: Pan MacMillan (UK), Ballantine Books (US)
ISBN (Various Editions):
  - Original UK Edition: ISBN 0-330-25864-8
  - US Edition: ISBN 0-345-39180-2
  - Modern editions may have different ISBNs
Genre: Science Fiction, Comedy
Format Options: Hardcover, Paperback, eBook, Audiobook


RESEARCH METHODOLOGY
--------------------

PHASE 1: ONLINE RETAILERS & MARKETPLACES
Priority: High | Timeline: Start with this phase

1.1 Major Online Retailers
    - Amazon.com / Amazon regional sites
    - Barnes & Noble (bnn.com)
    - Book Depository
    - Powell's Books
    - Better World Books

    Search Strategy: Use exact title and author name, filter by format and price
    Record: Availability, price range, shipping costs, condition (new/used)

1.2 Specialty Book Retailers
    - AbeBooks (specializes in rare/out-of-print books)
    - ViaLibri (meta-search for used books)
    - Alibris (rare and collectible books)
    - ThriftBooks (used copies)
    - eBay Books section

    Search Strategy: Compare used and vintage editions, note seller ratings
    Record: Edition details, condition reports, seller reliability


PHASE 2: LIBRARY SYSTEMS
Priority: High | Timeline: Parallel with Phase 1

2.1 Local Library Catalogs
    - Search your local library's OPAC (Online Public Access Catalog)
    - Check catalog holdings and current availability
    - Determine if copies can be placed on hold or reserved
    - Inquire about inter-library loan options

    Search Strategy: Check both physical and digital collections
    Record: Call number, location, availability dates, loan periods

2.2 Digital Library Services
    - OverDrive / Libby (eBook and audiobook lending)
    - Hoopla (digital library collection)
    - Other regional library digital services

    Search Strategy: Check if available for immediate digital borrowing
    Record: Format availability, wait lists, borrowing terms


PHASE 3: INDEPENDENT & SECONDHAND BOOKSELLERS
Priority: Medium | Timeline: If Phase 1-2 unsuccessful

3.1 Local Brick-and-Mortar Stores
    - Local independent bookstores
    - Used bookstores and vintage shops
    - Charity bookstores (Goodwill, Salvation Army, etc.)
    - University/college bookstores

    Search Strategy: Call ahead or visit in person
    Record: Store name, contact, availability, price, visit date

3.2 Online Used Book Marketplaces
    - WorldofBooks.com
    - VeryShelf
    - Swap.com
    - Local Buy/Sell/Trade groups (Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist)

    Search Strategy: Filter by condition and price range
    Record: Seller location, shipping timeline, price comparisons


PHASE 4: DIGITAL & ALTERNATIVE FORMATS
Priority: Medium | Timeline: Parallel search

4.1 eBook Platforms
    - Kindle (Amazon)
    - Apple Books
    - Google Play Books
    - Smashwords
    - Project Gutenberg (public domain check)

    Search Strategy: Compare eBook pricing across platforms
    Record: Price, format compatibility, DRM restrictions

4.2 Audiobook Sources
    - Audible
    - Google Play Books (audiobooks)
    - Scribd
    - Libro.fm

    Search Strategy: Check narration quality and reviews
    Record: Narrator, duration, pricing options


PHASE 5: DIRECT PUBLISHERS & SPECIAL EDITIONS
Priority: Low | Timeline: If special editions desired

5.1 Publisher Direct
    - Pan MacMillan UK official store
    - Hachette Book Group (US distributor)

    Search Strategy: Check for special/anniversary editions
    Record: Edition type, availability, exclusive features

5.2 Collector Editions
    - Deluxe/illustrated editions
    - Anniversary editions
    - Signed copies (through specialty dealers)

    Search Strategy: Research special releases and their availability
    Record: Edition details, rarity, estimated value


EVALUATION CRITERIA
-------------------
When comparing options, assess:

1. PRICE
   - Total cost including shipping/taxes
   - Comparison across multiple sources
   - Value for money vs. edition quality

2. AVAILABILITY
   - In stock vs. special order
   - Shipping timeline (standard vs. expedited)
   - Digital immediate vs. physical wait time

3. CONDITION
   - New vs. used condition rating
   - Binding quality and completeness
   - Pages intact and readable

4. FORMAT PREFERENCE
   - Physical book vs. digital preference
   - Audiobook vs. reading preference
   - Edition/cover preference

5. RELIABILITY
   - Seller/retailer ratings and reviews
   - Return policies
   - Customer service accessibility

6. ACCESSIBILITY
   - Local pickup option available
   - Accessibility features (large print, audio description)
   - Compatibility with devices/apps


RESEARCH DOCUMENTATION
----------------------
Create a comparison spreadsheet with columns for:
- Source/Retailer
- Format (Hardcover/Paperback/eBook/Audiobook)
- Edition
- Condition
- Price
- Shipping Cost
- Availability Date
- Total Cost
- Seller Rating
- URL
- Notes


TIMELINE
--------
Phase 1 & 2: Days 1-2 (Initial search and library check)
Phase 3: Days 3-5 (If needed, local store visits)
Phase 4: Days 1-2 (Parallel with other phases)
Phase 5: Days 5+ (Only if seeking special editions)


SUCCESS CRITERIA
----------------
Research is complete when:
✓ At least 5 viable sources have been identified
✓ Pricing comparison has been completed
✓ Availability timeline has been established
✓ Best value option has been selected based on criteria
✓ Purchase or loan has been initiated


CONTINGENCY PLAN
----------------
If standard sources unavailable:
- Check for special reprints or new editions planned
- Investigate rare book dealers for vintage editions
- Consider subscribing to books by author's works
- Check if unabridged audiobook is acceptable alternative
- Explore ebook trial services (Scribd, Kindle Unlimited)


NOTES
-----
- This book is widely available in multiple formats
- Multiple editions exist with different cover designs
- The complete Hitchhiker's series comprises 5 books
- Author Douglas Adams has passed away (2001)
- Book remains popular and continuously reprinted
- This book is commonly found in used bookstores
- Digital versions may be more cost-effective for budget-conscious searchers
