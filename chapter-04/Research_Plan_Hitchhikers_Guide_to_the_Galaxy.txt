================================================================================
RESEARCH PLAN: FINDING "THE HITCHHIKER'S GUIDE TO THE GALAXY"
================================================================================

OBJECTIVE:
Locate and acquire the book "The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy" by Douglas 
Adams, originally published in 1979.

================================================================================
RESEARCH PHASES
================================================================================

PHASE 1: INFORMATION GATHERING & VERIFICATION
---------------------------------------------
1.1 Confirm Book Details
    - Title: The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy
    - Author: Douglas Adams
    - Original Publication: 1979
    - ISBN-10: 0345391802 (Ballantine Books edition)
    - ISBN-13: 978-0345391803
    - Genre: Science Fiction, Comedy, Fantasy
    - Publisher: Pan Books (UK), Ballantine Books (US)

1.2 Identify Book Editions
    - Hardcover editions
    - Paperback editions
    - Collector's editions
    - Anniversary editions
    - eBook/digital editions
    - Audiobook versions
    - Foreign language editions


PHASE 2: LOCAL LIBRARY SYSTEMS
------------------------------
2.1 Public Library Research
    - Visit or call local public library
    - Search library catalog/website
    - Check availability in local branch
    - Place hold if currently checked out
    - Request interlibrary loan from other branches

2.2 Academic Libraries (if applicable)
    - University/college library systems
    - Alumni library access if available
    - Research library collections

2.3 Specialized Libraries
    - Science fiction collections
    - Children's/Young Adult collections (if available)


PHASE 3: PHYSICAL RETAIL SOURCES
--------------------------------
3.1 Brick-and-Mortar Bookstores
    - Major chains (Barnes & Noble, Waterstones, etc.)
    - Independent local bookstores
    - Used bookstores and vintage shops
    - Ask for in-store availability via phone

3.2 General Retailers
    - Walmart, Target, Costco
    - Drug store chains with book sections


PHASE 4: ONLINE RETAIL SOURCES
------------------------------
4.1 Major Online Retailers
    - Amazon.com (print and eBook)
    - Barnes & Noble (print and eBook)
    - Book Depository
    - Bookshop.org (supports independent bookstores)

4.2 Specialized Book Retailers
    - AbeBooks.com (used, rare, out-of-print books)
    - ThriftBooks
    - Powell's Books
    - Better World Books
    - ViaLibri (book search aggregator)

4.3 International Options
    - Local country-specific retailers
    - International book shipping services


PHASE 5: DIGITAL/eBook FORMATS
------------------------------
5.1 eBook Platforms
    - Amazon Kindle Store
    - Apple Books
    - Google Play Books
    - Kobo
    - Smashwords
    - Project Gutenberg (check if available - verify copyright status)

5.2 Subscription Services
    - Kindle Unlimited
    - Scribd
    - Audible Plus Catalog


PHASE 6: AUDIOBOOK SOURCES
--------------------------
6.1 Dedicated Audiobook Platforms
    - Audible.com
    - Libro.fm
    - Audiobooks.com

6.2 Library Audiobook Services
    - Libby/OverDrive (library-based)
    - Hoopla Digital (library-based)
    - Playaway
    - Kanopy


PHASE 7: ALTERNATIVE ACQUISITION METHODS
-----------------------------------------
7.1 Second-Hand Markets
    - eBay
    - Facebook Marketplace
    - Craigslist
    - Local community forums
    - Estate sales and auctions

7.2 Book Sharing/Lending
    - PaperBackSwap
    - BookMooch
    - Little Free Libraries (community book exchanges)
    - Ask friends/family to borrow


PHASE 8: SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS
-------------------------------
8.1 Edition Preferences
    - Determine desired format (hardcover, paperback, eBook, audio)
    - Consider special/collector's editions if interested
    - Check for anniversary editions with additional content

8.2 Price Comparison
    - Compare prices across multiple retailers
    - Look for discounts, sales, or coupon codes
    - Consider used book pricing vs. new

8.3 Shipping & Delivery
    - Check shipping costs and timeframes
    - Free shipping thresholds on various platforms
    - Prime shipping options if applicable
    - International shipping considerations


PHASE 9: VERIFICATION & AUTHENTICATION
--------------------------------------
9.1 Confirm Product Details
    - Verify edition/version matches expectations
    - Check condition descriptions for used books
    - Read seller reviews and ratings
    - Confirm publication details match requirements

9.2 Check Availability
    - Verify stock before purchase
    - Confirm estimated delivery dates
    - Check return/exchange policies


================================================================================
IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGY
================================================================================

IMMEDIATE STEPS (Days 1-2):
1. Check local library catalog online or in person
2. Search Amazon and major online retailers for current availability
3. Identify 2-3 retailers with best prices and delivery times

SHORT-TERM (Days 3-7):
4. Decide on preferred format and edition
5. Make purchase or place hold at library
6. Begin reading once received

CONTINGENCY OPTIONS:
7. If unavailable: Place order for used copy or eBook
8. If limited budget: Request through library system first
9. If urgent need: Consider eBook/audiobook for immediate access
